# Navier-Stokes global regularity: a transparent attempt that did not succeed

The Clay Millennium problem — statements (A), (B), (C), (D) of Fefferman's official description — is unsolved. **This attempt did not solve it and never came close.** There is no regularity theorem and no blowup construction anywhere in this directory. What is here is a record: five attack lines, each written out to the point where it stops, each then attacked by an adversarial referee, each revised so that every claim carries the status its weakest input allows, and every failure recorded with the exact step at which it breaks.

The motivation is Terence Tao's Mathstodon thread of 2026-09-03, which argued that a primarily AI-generated solution arriving as an opaque black box could "contaminate the problem as a source of further advances", because pure-mathematics problems are posed for the ideas the effort generates rather than for the yes/no answer. This project is an attempt written to the opposite standard: every claim carries a status tag, every computation is reproducible from a script in this directory, and the failures are recorded as precisely as the successes. The thread itself was reconstructed only from search snippets of its first two parts; the rest was never read, because `mathstodon.xyz` is blocked here. The user separately identified Tao's blog post of 2014-02-04 announcing the averaged Navier-Stokes blowup paper (arXiv:1402.0290, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 29 (2016) 601-674) as the approach they had in mind; that post is also blocked, its content was confirmed only through a snippet of the paper's abstract, and the project's framing of it — item 2.2 of `00_problem.md`, the source of transfer test T1 — was checked against that snippet and matches. Both remain unread primary sources.

## 1. The problem, in three sentences

For three-dimensional incompressible Navier-Stokes with fixed viscosity `nu > 0`, smooth divergence-free data and no force, it is not known whether the unique local smooth solution extends to all times, on `R^3` with Schwartz-type decay (statement (A)) or on the torus `T^3` (statement (B)); the breakdown statements (C) and (D) assert the opposite for some admissible forced data. The obstruction is that the only globally controlled coercive quantities — the energy and the cumulative dissipation — are supercritical: under the scaling `u_lambda(x,t) = lambda u(lambda x, lambda^2 t)` the energy carries a factor `lambda^(-1)` and so says less and less about fine scales, exactly where a singularity would live. The full statement as used here, with Fefferman's numbered hypotheses, a fifteen-item no-go checklist and six mandatory transfer tests, is `00_problem.md`.

## 2. Honest limits, before anything else

- **No full text of any cited paper was read anywhere in this project. Not one.** Read section 0 of `00_problem.md` before trusting any citation. The session's egress policy blocks every route to the literature: `arxiv.org`, `export.arxiv.org`, `doi.org`, `pnas.org`, `link.springer.com`, `sciencedirect.com`, `jstor.org`, `scholar.google.com`, `ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, `europepmc.org`, `api.semanticscholar.org`, `api.openalex.org`, `api.crossref.org`, `core.ac.uk`, `zenodo.org`, `hal.science`, `terrytao.wordpress.com`, `mathstodon.xyz`, `en.wikipedia.org` and `x.com` were all confirmed unreachable. A search tool returning titles, venues and occasionally one sentence of an abstract was the only external channel. A tag `[checked via WebSearch]` therefore confirms that a paper with that title, those authors and that venue exists — nothing else. It never means the theorem statement, its hypotheses or its scope were verified. Consequently no claim here may rest on the precise hypotheses of a cited theorem, and where an argument does depend on them the dependence is stated at the point of use and the claim carries the weaker status. Several claims below are weaker than the underlying mathematics may deserve for exactly this reason; `91_handoff_local.md` lists, in priority order, what a reader with library access should check first.
- **The novelty audit found nothing substantively new, and it did not cover the whole project.** `90_prior_art.md` graded 44 claims against the literature (through snippets only, so its own verdicts are weak evidence): 10 PUBLISHED, 21 FOLKLORE, 4 REDERIVED, 9 NEW-BUT-MINOR, and **0 NEW-AND-SUBSTANTIVE**. That is the expected and correct outcome for a Millennium problem. Its scope, stated in its own first paragraph, is `00_problem.md` and lines A, B and C as they stood at audit time: **line D did not yet exist, line E was a five-line skeleton, and `lean/` was not examined.** No claim of lines D or E has been graded for novelty at all; lines B and C were graded before their referee reports existed, so for them the audit describes an earlier text. The value claimed here is the artifact and its bookkeeping, not the mathematics.
- **No three-dimensional direct numerical simulation was run.** The session had 4 CPUs and no GPU. Every numerical claim is either a symbolic computation, exact rational arithmetic, a small spectral evaluation on a torus grid, or an ODE integration of a shell model. Nothing here is a fluid simulation, and no claim depends on one.
- **Only finite arithmetic is machine-checked.** `lean/Certificates.lean` proves 63 theorems in Lean 4.15.0 core, all by `decide` (kernel evaluation of closed arithmetic), with no `sorry`, no `native_decide` and no axiom dependence. It covers the triad transfer certificates of A2, the 2D negative control, the A3.1 enstrophy budget, two `L^4` thresholds and the A6 linear-algebra certificate. Within that narrow scope the coverage is uneven, and `lean/README.md` says so: the A2 transfers, the planar control and the A3.1 budget are recomputed inside Lean from the amplitudes, whereas the `L^4` fluxes and the five A6 matrices enter as the script's output and only their arithmetic is checked, so a wrong number there would be faithfully certified. The sign statements hold only at the integer amplitudes exhibited — core Lean has no reals, so "increases for every real `A > 64 pi`" is not expressible there, and that half of the A3.1 row below rests on the script, not on Lean. **The analytic core is not formalized at all** — no lemma of any line is a Lean theorem, and mathlib was unreachable. See `lean/README.md`, whose second half is a list of what is not formalized.
- **One session, one system, and the adversarial checking was done by the same family of system that produced the work.** The referee reports in `refutations/` are hostile reviews that reran every script with independent code and did find real defects — one issue flagged fatal and forty flagged major across the five reports, besides the minor ones; the consequential ones are recorded below and the reports carry the full lists — but they are not independent human refereeing, and a shared blind spot would be invisible to this process. Treat the whole directory as a claim to be checked, not as evidence.

## 3. Method

**Five attack lines**, chosen to cover the standard strategy space and each required to produce a concrete checkable object rather than a narrative:

- **A** (`line_a_lyapunov.md`, `line_a_lyapunov.py`) — is there a coercive Lyapunov functional whose monotonicity would force regularity?
- **B** (`line_b_selfsimilar.md`, `line_b_selfsimilar.py`) — self-similar blowup profiles, the Necas-Ruzicka-Sverak identity, and what exponent window survives.
- **C** (`line_c_cascade.md`, `line_c_cascade.py`) — can Tao's engineered-cascade mechanism be built in a dyadic shell model and ported to the true equation?
- **D** (`line_d_reduction.md`, `line_d_reduction.py`) — the critical `L^3` monotonicity method, its exact ceiling, and Liouville theorems for ancient solutions.
- **E** (`line_e_frontier.md`, `line_e_frontier.py`) — how far past the Lions exponent `5/4` a logarithmically supercritical hyperdissipative regularity argument can be pushed.

**Referee-then-revise.** Each line was written, then reviewed by a hostile referee that reran the script from scratch with independent code, re-derived proofs by hand, and reported issues as fatal, major or minor together with an *accepted status* for each criticized claim. The reports are in `refutations/`. Each line was then revised: no criticized claim was deleted, each was kept at the status the referee accepted, and where the referee's own positive control produced a new negative result it was written up as a claim in its own right (this is how B11 and the T2 failures of B and E arose). Nothing below is stated more strongly than its referee accepted.

**There was only one round, and the revisions are unrefereed.** No report in `refutations/` has seen the current text of any line file: each report reviews the round-1 version, and the revisions answering it were checked by their own authors and by the scripts, not by a second hostile reader. So the rows below divide into two kinds. Rows whose content the referee saw carry a status the referee accepted. Rows that exist only because of the revision — D3c(ii)'s exact cubic coefficient and the finiteness of the torus ceiling that follows from it, D7's rewritten Duhamel step, D5's corrected centering, C8's leave-one-out range, B10's closure of the `sigma = 5/4` endpoint inside the exact ansatz, B5-part5's rebuilt numerical check, B11, and the T2 paragraphs of B and E — have never been refereed at all, and a second reader should start there. The same applies to `lean/` and to `91_handoff_local.md`, which no report covers.

**Status vocabulary**, one tag per claim: **PROVED-HERE** (complete proof, every step checkable in the file), **KNOWN** (cited, not reproved — and, given section 0, cited without the hypotheses having been read), **COMPUTED** (script plus printed output), **CONJECTURE**, **FAILED** (attempted, with the exact breaking step recorded).

**Six transfer tests** (`00_problem.md` section 3), applied mechanically to every argument, with a per-line table of verdicts. T1: a regularity argument that applies verbatim to Tao's averaged equation is wrong, and must name the line where exact-nonlinearity structure is used. T2: a blowup mechanism that applies verbatim to a globally regular comparison system — 2D, swirl-free axisymmetric, hyperdissipative at order `5/4`, viscous Burgers, small `BMO^(-1)` data — is wrong. T3: consistency with all the known blowup-profile constraints simultaneously. T4: the inviscid-limit test against Elgindi's Euler blowup. T5: the weak-solution test against the non-uniqueness results. T6: the too-easy test — any step that would close a critical or subcritical estimate is presumed wrong until every constant is exhibited. T2 is where two lines were caught: running the swirl-free positive control that round 1 had logged "not applicable" broke a headline reading in B, and running T2 at all broke the evidential value of E's cascade budget.

## 5. The five lines, one paragraph each

**A — coercive Lyapunov functionals** (`line_a_lyapunov.md`, referee `refutations/line_a_r1_rigor.md`: not refuted, no fatal issue, one major and a cluster of minor issues, all dispositioned in the notes). Tried: decide whether regularity could follow from a functional of the velocity at a fixed time that never increases and controls a critical norm. Produced: Lemma A1 and six variants, converting "monotone under Navier-Stokes" into "conserved by 3D Euler" by amplitude scaling; two classifications of Euler invariants (radial Sobolev multipliers, zeroth-order local densities) with exact rational certificates that Lean re-checks; and an explicit trigonometric polynomial for each named candidate at which it strictly increases. The referee broke the `L^3` half of the A4 discussion (the shrinking-bump family did not prove what it was used for; a corrected family is written out) and forced the two sup-norm rows to be marked conditional on global maximality. Breakpoint: A1 converts the question into the classification of Euler invariants, and that classification is available here only for local zeroth-order and radial-quadratic functionals. For **nonlocal cubic** functionals — exactly where an `L^3`-scaling candidate would live — there is no classification and no method here; that gap is stated as conjecture (E). Even proving (E) would close only the monotone-state-functional route, not the problem: ESS-type control is about trajectories, and A1 says nothing about non-monotone a priori bounds. A1 also transfers verbatim to Tao's averaged equation, so by T1 it can never appear in a regularity proof — it is a filter, not a route.

**B — self-similar profiles** (`line_b_selfsimilar.md`, referee `refutations/line_b_r1.md`: not refuted, no fatal issue, seven major issues). Tried: pin down which self-similar blowup exponents survive all the standing constraints. Produced: a complete negative answer for the exact ansatz (B-exact); the NRS head-pressure identity verified symbolically in three arbitrary functions with falsifying controls; a self-contained Liouville theorem under three named hypotheses; the exact-ansatz hyperdissipative window with the Lions exponent falling out; and the CKN scaling exponents recovered numerically with the crossover produced rather than imposed. The referee broke four things: the headline "Type II, strictly faster than self-similar" was downgraded to KNOWN because its strict right endpoint rests entirely on an unread paper; the round-1 numerical support for the profile identity was shown to be vacuous (it checked the one integration by parts that does not use divergence-freeness) and was rebuilt; the "not applicable" verdict on the swirl-free positive control was wrong; and the reason the window is open rather than closed was downgraded to CONJECTURE. Breakpoint, in two places at once. First, B11: the same arithmetic gives the same nonempty window for swirl-free axisymmetric flow, which is globally regular — so **nothing downstream of "the window is nonempty" narrows the blowup problem**. Second, no smooth decaying Euler self-similar profile in the window is known to exist, this line produces none, and Chae's companion note arXiv:math/0601661 is reported to exclude locally self-similar singular solutions, which is precisely the surviving object; if its hypotheses reach into the window the line is refuted, and nobody here can check.

**C — engineered cascades** (`line_c_cascade.md`, referee `refutations/line_c_r1.md`: not refuted, but **one fatal issue** and eleven major ones). Tried: build Tao's self-replicating cascade in a dyadic shell model and see what blocks the port to the true equation. Produced: the exact energy identity and the scaling map of the model, symbolically; the unique base carrying the 3D Navier-Stokes scaling; a truncation-independent inviscid escape time; a stall law whose divergence point estimates the regularity threshold; a genuinely proved power-law comparison lemma; and an elementary proof that Turing-complete steady flows are excluded from the Clay class. The fatal issue was the regime dichotomy C10, tagged PROVED-HERE while being scaling heuristic with a false supporting "fact"; in revision it is split into the PROVED-HERE lemma and a CONJECTURE dichotomy, and everything that inherited it — the thresholds, the stall law, a transfer row — is retagged. The referee also showed the "global regularity is KNOWN" statement rests on an unread exponent range, that the reported `H^1` column measured a fixed grid time rather than the event, that the claimed one-percent threshold determination is a few-percent one, and that the sole sub-threshold escape data point is not truncation-independent after all. Breakpoint: the port. To carry the gadget over one must exhibit divergence-free wave packets whose *actual* Euler triad coefficients match the gadget's, and those coefficients are fixed by geometry. Individual coefficients do vanish, so it is false that specific interactions cannot be switched off; what is true is that they cannot be prescribed as a family — one chooses packets, not a table of numbers, and every wanted coefficient arrives with the unwanted ones its packets generate. Inside the ODE world the line stops earlier still: the gate itself was never built.

**D — conditional reductions** (`line_d_reduction.md`, referee `refutations/line_d_r1.md`: not refuted, no fatal issue, ten major issues). Tried: push the critical `L^3` monotonicity method to its exact ceiling, and prove the Liouville theorems that a Type I reduction would need. Produced: an exact `L^3` identity with every integration by parts justified in a named class; a small-data bound with explicit constants; the oddness dichotomy showing that finiteness of the ceiling is *equivalent* to the pressure work not vanishing identically; a proof that it does not vanish on `T^3`, with an exactly evaluated cubic coefficient; and two full Liouville theorems for bounded mild ancient solutions. The referee broke the advertised bracket on the ceiling (the upper number is a torus quantity and the truncation that would transfer it diverges, so **there is no upper bound at all on the whole-space ceiling**), the claim that no rescaling argument could enlarge the small-data set (not a mathematical statement, and false on its literal reading), the heading's account of which citations D2 depends on, the centering in the Type I reduction, and the justification of the Duhamel step in D7, which was reproved. Breakpoint: D2 step (iv). Dissipation and pressure work have identical homogeneity under both scalings, so progress requires bounding the pressure work *along trajectories*, not over fields. Downstream, both Liouville theorems need smallness of a scale-invariant quantity — removing it is essentially ESS — and even a complete Liouville theorem excludes only Type I.

**E — the logarithmic frontier** (`line_e_frontier.md`, referee `refutations/line_e_r1.md`: not refuted, no fatal issue, eleven major issues). Tried: push a hyperdissipative regularity argument as far past the Lions exponent as a weighted-enstrophy Gronwall scheme allows. Produced: the Lions a priori bound written out with every constant; an Osgood lemma converting a logarithmic loss into a divergence condition; the explicit Gronwall inequality the scheme delivers; and Proposition E6, showing that the weight cancels — for every weight in the admissible class the coefficient is bounded below by the same fourth power, so no weight helps. The referee broke the scope of E6 (proved for one scheme, under an unproved and deliberately cancellation-blind majorant, and quantitatively only for power weights), the claim that two independent estimates confirm the threshold (they share the decisive lossy substitution), the transfer table's "not applicable" for T2, a 2D claim whose stated proof does not exist at the endpoint, and an omitted numerical row that contradicts the reading given to its own table. Breakpoint: the scheme proves one inequality where it needs another, the two differing by exactly the squared loss that defines the absorption threshold — and that threshold is a per-frequency condition in which no weight appears. More importantly, **the whole line is about a modified equation**. Fefferman's problem fixes the true dissipation order, where the same cascade computation gives a finite drain `nu(2 + sqrt(2))` — and that identical number comes out for viscous Burgers and for swirl-free axisymmetric flow, both globally regular. The gap to the real problem is a full power of the frequency, not a logarithm, and nothing in this line bears on it.

## 6. If you want to falsify this, check these in order

1. **The one thing anyone can check with pen and paper in five minutes.** Item 2.3 of `00_problem.md` displays the *forward* self-similar profile equation, not the backward one: only the two drift terms carry the wrong sign. It matters, because a reader running B3's maximum-principle argument with that drift has a negative drift divergence and Step 2 fails. The erratum is written out in B1 of `line_b_selfsimilar.md` and has **not** been applied upstream; the line author did not own that file.
2. **Rerun the positive controls, because that is what broke two lines.** Apply B's window arithmetic to swirl-free axisymmetric flow (B11) and E's four cascade lines to 3D viscous Burgers (E4's T2 paragraph). Both comparison systems are globally regular. If you get the same answers the lines got, the corresponding "windows" and "budgets" are confirmed to carry no blowup information.
3. **The load-bearing unread citations**, in order of how much they carry. Chae, arXiv:math/0601661 — if it reaches locally self-similar solutions in the window, line B is refuted outright. Barbato-Morandin-Romito 2011 and 2014 — they carry C7's global-regularity conjecture *and* the entire target frame of line E. Iwaniec-Martin's `L^3` Riesz-transform norm — it carries D's headline numbers. Necas-Ruzicka-Sverak and Tsai's decay bootstrap — whether their proofs factor through B3's hypotheses. Tao 2016 — every claim about what the averaged operator preserves, hence the sharpness of T1 itself. Serre 1984 — whether A6 really is subsumed. Enciso-Peralta-Salas-Torres de Lizaur — whether their hypotheses cover the setting, which would settle a sub-case of (E). `91_handoff_local.md` has the same list with the specific hypothesis to check in each case.
4. **Rerun everything.** The five scripts are deterministic, need only numpy, scipy, sympy and mpmath, and run in between about 18 seconds and about 3 minutes each on 4 CPUs; each line's "How to reproduce" section gives the command and says which printed block each table is. `lean Certificates.lean` with Lean 4.15.0 must exit 0 and print exactly 63 audit lines, in about ten seconds.
5. **Re-check the tags against the papers.** `90_prior_art.md` already found two misattributions in line A that had survived a full referee round, and its own verdicts rest on snippets. Assume more are wrong.

## 7. Reading it in Tao's terms

What is **digestible** — usable by someone else without trusting this project's judgement — is the negative and infrastructural half. The fifteen-item no-go checklist and the six transfer tests of `00_problem.md` are a reusable filter, and applying them mechanically is what caught the two worst errors here; they cost nothing to adopt. The exact rational certificates (A2's triad determinant against the vanishing 2D control, A3.1's budget, A6's `det = 7936`) are checkable in isolation, and `lean/Certificates.lean` re-checks them, and the surrounding bookkeeping, in 63 kernel-evaluated theorems that depend on no axioms. B-exact is a clean negative result, complete except for its last step, which is the cited NRS theorem. D1's `L^3` identity and D3c(i)'s oddness dichotomy are small, complete and reusable: the dichotomy in particular reduces "is there a finite ceiling for the `L^3` monotonicity method" to a single sign question. B11 and E's T2 paragraph are worth reading as method: both are cases where running a positive control that had been logged "not applicable" destroyed the line's headline reading. What is **not** digestible is everything tagged CONJECTURE for citation reasons — those claims are placeholders for a reader with library access, not results.

The next steps that follow from the breakpoints rather than from wishful thinking, in the order the breakpoints suggest:

- **A.** Classify all trilinear symmetric forms on divergence-free fields, with Fourier kernels supported on zero-sum triples, that are conserved by 3D Euler. This is a finite linear-algebra problem on each quartet of wavevectors, the existing exact triad machinery is the right tool, and a negative answer closes the monotone route for every polynomial functional of `L^3` scaling. Note first that the diffeomorphism-invariant sub-case may already be settled in the literature.
- **B.** Read Chae's companion note before anything else — it may close the window and refute the line. If it does not: define "asymptotically self-similar" (topology, region, uniformity) and prove the correction subordinate in the four norms actually used, which is what B5.5 says is missing; and settle the `beta = 2/5` endpoint, which needs a non-concentration statement for suitable weak solutions at a first singular time that is not in the no-go list.
- **C.** Compute, for one concrete family of divergence-free wave packets, the ratio of unwanted to wanted Euler triad coefficients. That number is the whole question of whether the gadget ports, individual coefficients are known to vanish, and no bound on the ratio exists anywhere here. Separately, build the gate in the ODE world first — it was never built.
- **D.** Bound the pressure work along trajectories rather than over fields; D2 step (iv) is exactly where the two homogeneities coincide, and that is the only place progress can come from. In parallel, settle the whole-space transference conjecture, or compute the pressure work directly for a compactly supported field in a box several periods wide and watch the sign stabilise under box refinement.
- **E.** Prove the diagonal trilinear majorant (D) off the diagonal, or abandon the split-at-`N_0` scheme for the shell-flux iteration sketched in E8, whose high-high-to-low flux term is the unwritten part. But the honest reading of this line is its own last sentence: the distance from a logarithmically supercritical result to Fefferman's problem is a full power of the frequency, and no amount of sharpening inside this frame reduces it.

Nothing in the list above is a plan to solve the problem. They are the specific next things this attempt would have done, written down so that the failures are as reusable as the successes.
