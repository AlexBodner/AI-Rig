## 1. Headline answer

Essentially all of the mathematics in this project is reproduction of results that are published, textbook, or expert folklore, and this is the expected and correct outcome for a Millennium problem: the project contains no regularity theorem, no blowup construction, and nothing that moves the Clay problem. The one structural claim that carries the project — Lemma A1, that a differentiable positively-homogeneous functional which never increases along Navier-Stokes solutions must have vanishing Euler flux — is a folklore observation whose content is the standard reason the published Lyapunov-function literature for Navier-Stokes (Kato; Cannone-Planchon) only ever asserts monotonicity *under a smallness hypothesis*, and whose consequence ("the Euler invariants are not coercive") is stated in words in Tao's 2007 supercriticality post. Its classification consequences are weaker than Serre's published 1984 theorem in the local case (A6 is strictly subsumed by it, which the notes do not say) and its refutation certificates (A2, A3) re-derive facts — enstrophy grows, critical norms grow — that are the subject of an entire published numerical-optimization literature (Lu-Doering; Ayala-Protas; Bleitner-Protas). Line B is a digestion of Leray, Necas-Ruzicka-Sverak, Tsai, Chae-Wolf and Seregin's Euler-scaling program; line C is a digestion of Katz-Pavlovic, Cheskidov and the K41 fixed point of dyadic models. The genuine residue is a handful of easy technical variants of A1 (A1b, A1c, A1c', A1d, A1f, A1g), one worked candidate (A5), and one numerical estimate (C8) — none of them research-level, all of them one-step consequences of standard technique. What *is* real, and should not be undersold, is the artifact rather than the theorem: a self-consistent, machine-checkable, adversarially refereed write-up in which every claim carries a status tag, every certificate is exact-rational and reproducible, and every failure is recorded with its breaking step. That is precisely the transparency Tao's thread asks for, and a REDERIVED verdict below is a statement about the mathematics, not about the value of the exercise.

## 3. Closest prior work

**A1 (amplitude-scaling lemma).** Nearest published items, in decreasing order of closeness.

- *T. Kato's local Lyapunov property*, and *M. Cannone and F. Planchon, "Fonctions de Lyapunov pour les equations de Navier-Stokes", Seminaire Equations aux derivees partielles (Ecole Polytechnique), 1999-2000, expose no. 11, 7 pp.* [checked via WebSearch: authors, title, seminar, year, expose number and page count from the Numdam record; a companion "More Lyapunov functions for the Navier-Stokes equations" is reported by a secondary source but I could not confirm its publication data]. Kato showed that many norms (`L^p` for `1 < p < infinity`, `W^{s,p}` for `s > 0`) decrease monotonically **provided the solution is small in `L^m`** [checked via WebSearch: paraphrase quoted in section 5]; Cannone-Planchon extended this to Besov norms. *What differs:* this is the positive half, under a smallness hypothesis; A1 is the negative half, and says the smallness hypothesis is unavoidable for any candidate whose Euler flux does not vanish. Nobody in this literature appears to state that converse as a lemma, but the shape of their theorems shows they knew it. This is the single strongest reason to grade A1 FOLKLORE rather than NEW. <!-- codespell:ignore fonctions -->
- *T. Tao, "Why global regularity for Navier-Stokes is hard" (2007)* [checked via WebSearch: post exists; two phrases quoted verbatim in section 5]. Supplies the conclusion A1 aims at — that the Euler-conserved structure is "very far from coercive, being almost purely topological in nature" — but as an assertion, without the amplitude-scaling reduction that derives it from monotonicity under Navier-Stokes. *What differs:* Tao asserts non-coerciveness of the known invariants; A1 proves that monotonicity forces you into that class in the first place. That step, small as it is, is the one thing in line A I could not find written down anywhere.
- Norm-inflation constructions (Bourgain-Pavlovic in `Bdot^{-1}_{inf,inf}` and its descendants) use the same "make the amplitude large so the quadratic term dominates" mechanism to defeat continuity of the solution map [unverified: I recall the mechanism but confirmed no snippet describing it in these terms].

**A2 and A6 (classification of invariants).** *D. Serre, "Les invariants du premier ordre de l'equation d'Euler en dimension trois", Physica D 13 (1984) 105-136* [checked via WebSearch: journal, volume, pages, DOI 10.1016/0167-2789(84)90273-2, and the statement]. Every first-order invariant of 3D incompressible Euler is a linear combination of momenta, energy and helicity. *What differs:* A6 is the zeroth-order sub-case and follows immediately (a `phi(u)` density is first-order); A2's `s = 1` case (enstrophy) likewise. A2's general real `s` is genuinely outside Serre's reach because `Hdot^s` is nonlocal, and there the triad certificate is doing real work — but the conclusion is one an expert would assume. The project's own Breakpoint section misstates the relation by saying nothing of Serre is reproved here.

**Conjecture (E).** *A. Enciso, D. Peralta-Salas and F. Torres de Lizaur, "Helicity is the only integral invariant of volume-preserving transformations", PNAS 113 (2016) 2035-2040* [checked via WebSearch: authors, title, venue, volume, pages, and the statement; also indexed as settling the Arnold-Khesin conjecture, with a related "helicity uniqueness conjecture in 3d hydrodynamics" manuscript by Khesin and coauthors seen in the same results]. Any regular functional on exact divergence-free fields on a compact 3-manifold that is invariant under all volume-preserving diffeomorphisms is a function of the helicity. *What differs:* invariance under all volume-preserving diffeomorphisms is strictly stronger than invariance along the Euler flow, so this does not prove (E); but it does prove (E) for the diffeomorphism-invariant (Casimir) sub-class, since a function of helicity is not coercive over `L^3`. Any serious attempt on (E) should start from this theorem and from Tao's 2014 posts "Conserved quantities for the Euler equations" and "Noether's theorem, and the conservation laws for the Euler equations" [checked via WebSearch: both posts exist, dated 2014-02-25 and 2014-03-02]. None of these appear in the project's references. A cautionary datum from the same corner of the literature: arXiv:1007.5369, "New Conserved Quantities of the Incompressible Euler Equations", was **withdrawn** by its author after the quantities were pointed out to be topological-degree restatements [checked via WebSearch: withdrawal and reason].

## 4. What the project does NOT claim

A reader skimming the status tags could over-read them. To be explicit, and consistent with what the notes themselves say:

- **Nothing here proves or disproves global regularity**, in any of Fefferman's four statements. There is no regularity theorem and no blowup construction anywhere in the repository.
- **A1 does not say "there is no Lyapunov functional".** It says that within four explicitly delimited classes (differentiable and absolutely homogeneous; odd-degree; with an asymptotic top-degree derivative; convex and Hadamard-differentiable) a monotone functional must be Euler-conserved. Cubic and higher nonlocal functionals, first-order local densities, and anything outside those hypotheses are untouched — and the notes say the cubic case is exactly where an `L^3`-scaling candidate would live.
- **A1 does not rule out a priori bounds.** It constrains *monotone* state functionals only. Every actual 3D regularity mechanism (hyperdissipative Gronwall arguments, small data, the Escauriaza-Seregin-Sverak route) is of the bounded-but-non-monotone or trajectory type and is untouched; A4 says so at length.
- **A1 cannot appear in a regularity proof.** It transfers verbatim to Tao's averaged equation, which blows up. It is a filter, not a route.
- **A2 does not classify all quadratic Euler invariants.** The single triad is silent about symbols agreeing on that triad, and about non-radial or matrix-valued symbols (where helicity lives).
- **A3.3 is not certified.** The two sup-norm rows depend on a numerically located maximizer being global; the Lipschitz-certificate grid resolution needed is computed and was not run, and no interval arithmetic was performed.
- **Conjecture (E) is a conjecture**, and by the PNAS 2016 result above the nearby Casimir question is already settled in the literature, which the notes do not mention.
- **Line B produces no profile.** The window `2/5 <= beta < 1/2` is a constraint satisfied by the empty set for all anyone knows; no Euler self-similar profile in it is known to exist, and B7.3 explains why the formal perturbative argument does not upgrade to shadowing.
- **Line C is about an ODE model, not a fluid.** Its Breakpoint 2 states plainly that the Euler triad coefficients are not free parameters, which is the reason the construction does not port.
- **The certificates are exact but the environment is not a proof assistant.** "COMPUTED (exact)" means exact rational arithmetic in a Python script that was rerun by an adversarial referee with independent code; it does not mean formally verified.
