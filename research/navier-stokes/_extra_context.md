Confirmed content: Navier-Stokes is written `∂_t u = Δu + B(u,u)` with `B` bilinear on divergence-free fields obeying the cancellation `⟨B(u,u),u⟩ = 0`, which is equivalent to the energy identity. Tao replaces `B` by an averaged operator `B̃`, where the average involves rotations and Fourier multipliers of order zero, and which obeys the same cancellation and hence the same energy identity. By analyzing a system of ODEs related to the dyadic Navier-Stokes model of Katz and Pavlovic, he constructs a smooth solution of the averaged equation that blows up in finite time. The stated consequence is that any positive resolution of the three-dimensional global regularity problem must use finer structure of the nonlinearity `B(u,u)` than is provided by harmonic analysis estimates together with the energy identity.

This is exactly item 2.2 of `00_problem.md` and the source of transfer test T1, and it is the subject of attack line C.
